Blood Donation Requirements

Check these general eligibility conditions before donating. Final eligibility is determined by the medical team.

Age Weight Hemoglobin Level Blood Pressure Condition of Body
18 years or above Generally 50 kg or above At least 12.5 g/dL Systolic: 100–140 mmHg
Diastolic: 60–90 mmHg 		Should be medically fit.
Recent illness, tattoos and other factors must be assessed by the medical team.
